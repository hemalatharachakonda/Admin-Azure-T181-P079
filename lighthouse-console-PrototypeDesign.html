Lighthouse Console

A working mockup of the delegated-access model from our design study — see exactly what a provider can reach in each customer tenant, check a role/scope pairing before granting it, and view activity across every tenant in one place.

Every customer tenant we manage, and exactly what our access looks like in each one. Scope is fixed at the resource-group level wherever possible — the wider the scope, the higher the risk if that delegation is ever misused.

Before granting a delegation, check what it actually exposes. Pick a role and a scope the way you would in the onboarding template — the console flags the blast radius before you deploy anything.

Each tenant keeps its own activity log by default — this view is what our Resource Graph + Sentinel layer produces on top: one merged timeline, still tagged by source tenant.
